What døPe supports

døPe is designed for users who want a high-control music experience on iOS. The app focuses on imported audio, Apple Music library access where available, detailed song metadata, synchronized lyrics, and customizable visual themes.

  • Imported music: Bring audio files into the app and browse them through albums, artists, playlists, songs, and cover flow.
  • Apple Music access: If you authorize Apple Music, døPe can surface music tied to your own Apple Music account and library access.
  • Now Playing controls: Playback controls include track navigation, progress control, lyrics viewing, rating states, shuffle, repeat, and volume modes.
  • Customization: Users can choose preset device colors, create custom themes, and save multiple color combinations directly on-device.
  • Metadata and artwork: døPe supports metadata edits, album artwork updates, and date-added style sorting for imported and Apple Music library content where available.

Frequently asked questions

How do I import my own music files?

Use the in-app music import flow to pick audio files from the Files app. Imported tracks are then indexed in the app library and become available under albums, artists, songs, playlists, and other browsing views.

Does døPe support Apple Music?

Yes. If you grant Apple Music access and have an active Apple Music setup on your device, døPe can use Apple frameworks to surface Apple Music content tied to your account and library access.

Why does a song appear without artwork or full metadata?

Imported audio quality varies by source. Some files contain incomplete tags. You can update metadata and artwork from within the app, and artwork availability may differ between imported local files and Apple Music-backed content.

How does sorting by date added work?

For imported files, døPe uses the best available added-date information from the source file and import time. For Apple Music library items, døPe uses the date the item was added to the user’s Apple Music library when that native library metadata is available.

Where are my custom themes stored?

Custom themes are stored locally on your device. You can create, save, edit, and delete user-created themes inside the app. Default built-in themes remain protected.

What should I send if I report a bug?

Send the device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app build number, what screen you were on, whether the issue involves imported music or Apple Music, and the exact steps to reproduce the problem.

Privacy

døPe is designed as a local-first music app. Imported files, playlists, theme settings, metadata edits, and lyrics-related app data are primarily stored on-device. Apple Music authorization and playback behavior follow Apple’s own platform rules when you choose to enable that integration.
